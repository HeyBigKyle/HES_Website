A good starting point

Before you call.

For audio video repair inquiries, gather a few details about the system so we can assess the issue and discuss service options. Please leave equipment cases closed and avoid handling electrical wiring.

  • Equipment brands and model numbers
  • Exact error messages or photos
  • When it last worked and what changed

Isolate the symptom

Check the selected input, volume, and mute setting. If possible, try a different source. Knowing whether one source or the whole system is affected helps narrow the conversation.

Retrace the change

An outage, a new device, or an altered setting can provide a clue. For intermittent issues, record the room, source, and time rather than relying on “it sometimes stops.”

Preserve the settings

Avoid factory resets unless the manufacturer or installer recommends one. They can erase system configuration. Tell us what you have already tried so we can discuss a sensible next step.

Ask about support

Describe the symptom, when it started, and what changed. We’ll assess the issue and talk through the service options — no pressure, no jargon.

Questions

Common questions

The screen says no signal — is my TV broken?

Probably not. It is usually the selected input, a source device, or a cable. Check the input and try a different source, then tell us the equipment models and exact messages so we can narrow it down.

Should I factory reset my receiver or TV?

Avoid it unless the manufacturer or installer recommends it — factory resets erase system configuration. Tell us what you have already tried instead.

Do you service systems another company installed?

Yes. Send the equipment brands and model numbers, photos, and a description of the problem, and we will assess the issue and discuss service options.

My problem only happens sometimes — can you still help?

Yes. For intermittent issues, record the room, the source, and the time each occurrence happens. That pattern is often the best clue we have.

How do I start a repair request?

Gather the details above — models, messages or photos, when it last worked, and what changed — then call (844) 437-4621 or send them through the contact page.

Describe the problem, what changed, and what you have tried. HES will assess it and discuss the right next step.
