Questions

Common questions in Lynnwood

Do you serve Lynnwood?

Yes. HES is a locally owned Greater Seattle integrator serving Lynnwood and the surrounding area. Brian Ritchie has been designing and installing home entertainment and smart home systems here since 2003 — and in AV since 1988.

Something stopped working — what do I do first?

Write down the symptom before touching anything: what stopped, when, and which devices are affected. A missing picture from one source is a different problem than a display that never turns on — the details point us to the fix.

Should I factory-reset the device that’s acting up?

Not yet. Factory resets can erase working configuration and make diagnosis harder. Bring model numbers, photos and a list of recent changes, and we’ll review the system with you first.

What should I send Brian before a visit?

A useful brief includes: The exact symptom or error message, When the system last worked, Photos and model numbers. The more you include up front, the more useful the first conversation — we review the property and compatibility before recommending equipment.

Can you work with the equipment I already have?

Often, yes. HES starts by reviewing what you own — make, model numbers and how it's connected — before recommending anything new. Working components stay; only what holds the experience back gets replaced.
