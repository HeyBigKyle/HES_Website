A good starting point

Start with the listening zones.

A zone is a space you want to control independently. Getting those boundaries right matters more than putting a speaker in every room. We start by mapping:

  • Spaces that should play together
  • Rooms that need independent volume
  • Music services and existing audio equipment

Follow the way you move

An open kitchen and dining area may suit one listening zone. A bedroom or office often benefits from separate control. We map the spaces before deciding how many components the system needs.

Give speakers the right home

Freestanding speakers offer flexibility; in-wall and in-ceiling options keep surfaces clear. Placement, room size, and furniture influence how evenly music reaches the listening area. Outdoor areas get weather-rated speakers that stand up to the Pacific Northwest.

Keep your music close

Sonos is among the audio brands HES carries, alongside architectural options for a cleaner look. Your preferred music services, household devices, and existing equipment help determine how you select sources and adjust the volume.

Questions

Common questions

What is a listening zone?

A zone is a space you control independently — the kitchen and porch can play together while a bedroom stays quiet, for example. Getting zone boundaries right matters more than speaker count.

Can you put speakers outdoors?

Yes — outdoor-rated speakers are built for Northwest weather, and the porch or patio can be its own zone with independent volume.

Can I keep my existing speakers or sound system?

Usually. Tell us the brands, model numbers, and the music services you use, and we will design around what is worth keeping.

Do I need to open walls for whole-home audio?

Not always. In-wall and in-ceiling speakers are ideal during construction or remodels, but wireless and architectural options let us add music to finished rooms without tearing them up.

Which brands do you install for multiroom audio?

We carry Sonos, Bowers & Wilkins, and Sonance among others — wireless for flexibility, architectural speakers for a clean look — and recommend based on your rooms and budget.

Where does music belong in your home?

Kitchen, porch, office, everywhere — tell us how you listen and HES will map the zones and the speakers to match.
