A good starting point

The room sets the brief.

A dedicated theater and a family living room ask different things of a system. Daylight, seating, and how you use the space guide the design. Before recommending equipment, HES looks at:

  • How far you sit from the screen
  • Movies, sports, gaming — or everyday television
  • Equipment you want to keep

Picture that fits

A television can suit a bright, shared room; projection calls for a closer look at light control and screen placement. We consider viewing positions, TV mounting conditions, and projector installation requirements before narrowing the options.

Hear the conversation

Speaker placement shapes dialogue and surround effects. A soundbar, architectural speakers, or a dedicated surround layout should be chosen for the room’s geometry and the listening experience you want — not from a box on a shelf.

Keep the controls within reach

Sources, displays, and receivers need to work together. We plan cable access, equipment ventilation, and daily operation so the technical choices support the way you watch — one remote, no cheat sheet.

Questions

Common questions

Should I choose a TV or a projector?

A television suits bright, shared rooms; projection shines when light can be controlled and the seating distance supports a larger image. We review daylight, screen placement, and mounting conditions before narrowing the options.

Can you build a theater in a living room instead of a dedicated space?

Yes. A family living room asks different things of a system than a dedicated theater, but clear dialogue, an engaging picture, and surround sound are all achievable — the design simply follows the room’s geometry and light.

Can we keep our existing receiver or speakers?

Often, yes. Send us the brands and model numbers before the visit and we will review compatibility with new sources and controls — the goal is a coherent system, not replacing every component by default.

Will I need several remotes to run everything?

No. Sources, displays, and receivers are planned to work together from one set of controls, and we walk you through it before we leave — movie night is one button, not a handful of remotes.

Where do you install home theaters?

Across Greater Seattle — Seattle, the Eastside, and surrounding communities — for locally owned homes and second homes. We have been installing in this region since 2003.

What does your movie night look like?

Tell us about the room, the screen, and the sound you want. HES will design a theater around it — and you will only ever need one remote.
