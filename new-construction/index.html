Plan the details

The wiring brief follows how you will live.

Start with the furniture, screens, and desks

Mark where people will sit, watch, and work. A room label alone does not establish the right television height, speaker position, or network outlet. Include outdoor listening areas and future uses in the discussion so the wiring brief follows how you will live.

Coordinate before finishes close

Cable routes, speaker locations, and equipment storage are easier to evaluate while access is available. Share the build stage and the dates for relevant work so HES can discuss installation timing with you and the project team. The scope and schedule need to be agreed for the actual build.

Give equipment a serviceable home

A receiver, network switch, or amplifier needs more than a shelf. Consider ventilation, power, cable entry, and access for connections. Clear identification and an accessible equipment location help future troubleshooting and upgrades.

Plan the first day of use

Prewiring is one stage; fitting speakers, connecting sources, and configuring controls comes later. Discuss the intended handover, components you plan to retain, and who will use the system. Product choices and wiring requirements should be reviewed together.

Before we visit

Bring the details that shape the work. A useful first conversation starts with the property and the work already planned.

  • Current drawings and furniture layouts
  • Construction stage and expected milestones
  • Rooms for audio, television, work, and networking
  • Existing components to include

Questions

Common questions

When should we bring HES into a new construction project?

As early as possible — while plans and cable routes can still be reviewed. If construction is already underway, share the current stage and we will identify the remaining options.

What do you need from us before the first conversation?

Current drawings and furniture layouts, the construction stage and milestones, which rooms need audio, television, and networking, and any existing components you want to include.

Do you coordinate with our builder?

Yes. We discuss installation timing with you and the project team, and the scope and schedule are agreed for the actual build before finishes close.

Is prewiring the whole job?

No — prewiring is one stage. Fitting speakers, connecting sources, and configuring controls come later, around handover. We plan both stages together so nothing gets missed.

Can you include outdoor audio and future uses?

Absolutely. We include outdoor listening areas and future uses in the wiring brief so the home is ready when you are.

Let’s talk through your plans.

Send the drawings and the construction schedule. HES will review them and coordinate with your project team so the wiring brief follows how you will live.
