A good starting point

Name the moments first.

HES works with Lutron lighting solutions. Useful scenes begin with the activities in your home, then the fixtures and controls that can support them. We start with:

  • Fixtures used for cooking, reading, and relaxing
  • Switch locations that feel awkward today
  • Lamps, drivers, and dimming requirements

Build scenes around activities

A scene recalls a combination of light levels. Rather than starting with a long list of presets, identify the few settings you would actually use: dinner, reading, or the end of the evening.

Make wall controls make sense

A keypad can bring several settings together, while a familiar wall control remains useful for guests. The controls you touch every day should be easy to find and understand.

Check the dimming details

LED lamps, drivers, and fixtures respond differently to dimmers. Wiring conditions and product compatibility need to be reviewed before specifying controls or promising a particular dimming result.

Talk about lighting

Tell us which rooms fight you and which moments matter. We’ll plan scenes, controls, and dimming that suit the way you live.

Questions

Common questions

What is a lighting scene?

A scene recalls a combination of light levels at once — dinner, reading, or the end of the evening, for example. We start with the few settings you would actually use rather than a long list of presets.

Will guests be able to use the lights?

Yes. Alongside keypads that group several settings, familiar wall controls remain available, so no one needs an app or an explanation to turn on a light.

Can you fix LED flicker or buzzing?

Often, yes. LED lamps, drivers, and fixtures respond differently to dimmers; we review wiring conditions and product compatibility before specifying controls so dimming performs as promised.

Do lighting controls work with shades and entertainment?

They can share controls when the products and installation conditions support it — movie time can dim the lights and lower the shades together. We review compatibility before recommending which systems work together.

Do I need to rewire my home for lighting control?

Not necessarily. Lutron lighting solutions include options for existing homes as well as new construction; we evaluate your wiring conditions first and recommend from there.

How would you like the room to feel?

Name the moments — dinner, reading, the end of the evening — and HES will build the lighting around them.
