A good starting point

Coverage is not the same as speed.

An internet plan and a home’s internal network solve different problems. Tell us where performance changes, rather than only the speed on the bill:

  • Rooms where connections drop
  • Your modem, router, and internet plan
  • Fixed devices that could use wired connections

Find the weak spots

Building materials, distance, and equipment placement affect wireless coverage. Note which rooms and activities are affected, and whether the issue changes with the time of day or the number of users.

Put fixed equipment on the plan

Offices, televisions, and media equipment may benefit from wired connections. During a build or remodel, consider routes to these locations and an accessible place for network equipment.

Choose equipment for the property

HES lists eero, Luxul, and Netgear among its networking brands. The floor plan, device count, and available wiring guide the configuration; adding more hardware alone does not define a network design.

Review your network

Describe where the connection falls short and what you’re paying for. We’ll diagnose the real bottleneck and plan a network that holds up.

Questions

Common questions

My speed test is fine — why does video still buffer?

A speed test measures your internet plan, not your home’s internal network. Coverage, building materials, distance, and equipment placement decide what actually reaches each room.

Should I just add more mesh units?

Not always. Adding hardware without a plan can make things worse. We look at the floor plan, device count, and wiring first, then configure eero, Luxul, or Netgear equipment for the property.

Which devices should be wired instead of wireless?

Fixed equipment — offices, televisions, media players — benefits most from wired connections, freeing wireless capacity for phones and laptops. During a build or remodel, we plan routes and an accessible equipment location.

Do you fix existing Wi-Fi problems?

Yes. Wi-Fi troubleshooting is part of the work — tell us which rooms drop and when, and whether it changes with the time of day or the number of users, and we will find the weak spots.

Does the network affect my home theater and audio?

Directly. Streaming, video calls, and whole-home music all ride the same network, so a shaky foundation shows up everywhere. We design the network as part of the entertainment plan, not as an afterthought.

Where does your connection fall short?

Tell us which rooms drop, what slows down, and when. HES will find the weak spots and design a network around your home.
