Questions

Common questions in Everett

Do you serve Everett?

Yes. HES is a locally owned Greater Seattle integrator serving Everett and the surrounding area. Brian Ritchie has been designing and installing home entertainment and smart home systems here since 2003 — and in AV since 1988.

How many audio zones do I actually need?

As many as your habits need. Rooms used together can share a zone; spaces like bedrooms or offices usually want independent volume. List the places you listen and the zones design themselves around the household.

My music streams drop out — do I need new speakers?

Probably not — streaming dropouts are usually a network issue, not a speaker issue. Tell us where music cuts out and we’ll review connectivity alongside the audio plan, so the fix matches the real cause.

What should I send Brian before a visit?

A useful brief includes: Listening rooms and music services, Which spaces need separate volume, Any streaming dropouts. The more you include up front, the more useful the first conversation — we review the property and compatibility before recommending equipment.

Can you work with the equipment I already have?

Often, yes. HES starts by reviewing what you own — make, model numbers and how it's connected — before recommending anything new. Working components stay; only what holds the experience back gets replaced.
