Plan the details

The renovation opens doors — plan what goes through them.

Separate open walls from finished spaces

Identify the walls, ceilings, and cabinetry being changed. That shows where new wiring may be practical and where the plan needs to work around existing finishes. Include adjacent rooms if they share sources, controls, or network equipment.

Set positions before closing the details

Review furniture, display height, speaker locations, and shade clearance before finalizing surfaces. A new built-in or fireplace surround can affect mounting, access, and cable entry. Discuss those decisions while the design can still respond.

Decide what earns a place in the new room

An existing receiver, television, or speaker system may be worth retaining. Send model numbers so HES can review compatibility with new sources and controls. The goal is a coherent system rather than replacing every component by default.

Plan disruption and the return to use

Tell us which spaces need to remain usable during the renovation. Agree how removed equipment will be handled, what access is needed, and which connections require checking when the room is ready. Installation timing depends on the work being completed around it.

Before we visit

Bring the details that shape the work. A useful first conversation starts with the property and the work already planned.

  • Which finishes will be removed or retained
  • Models of equipment you want to keep
  • Rooms that need to stay usable

Questions

Common questions

Can AV work fit into a partial remodel?

Often, yes — but the available access defines the options. Share exactly which areas are changing so we can assess whether the desired wiring and positions are practical.

What do you need before the first conversation?

The renovation drawings or a written scope, which finishes will be removed or retained, the models of equipment you want to keep, and which rooms need to stay usable.

Should we replace our existing receiver and speakers?

Not by default. Send the model numbers and we will review compatibility with new sources and controls — the goal is a coherent system, not replacing everything.

Can you work around spaces we need to keep usable?

Yes. Tell us which rooms must remain livable during the renovation and we will plan access, timing, and the return to use around them.

Does a new fireplace surround or built-in affect the AV plan?

It can — mounting, access, and cable entry all change with new built-ins. That is why we review display height, speaker locations, and shade clearance before surfaces are finalized.
