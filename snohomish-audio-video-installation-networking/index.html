Questions

Common questions in Snohomish

Do you serve Snohomish?

Yes. HES is a locally owned Greater Seattle integrator serving Snohomish and the surrounding area. Brian Ritchie has been designing and installing home entertainment and smart home systems here since 2003 — and in AV since 1988.

Can you add speakers and wiring to a finished home?

That’s our retrofit specialty. Concealed wiring depends on structure and reachable pathways — attic, crawlspace, existing wire — so we review what your home allows first, then discuss alternatives where the ideal route isn’t practical.

Will the installation damage my finishes?

We plan routes to protect them. Speaker and screen positions are balanced against the existing finishes, and photographs plus a visit help establish a realistic, finish-respecting scope before any work starts.

What should I send Brian before a visit?

A useful brief includes: Photos of the rooms and finishes, Known wiring and accessible spaces, Where equipment should be stored. The more you include up front, the more useful the first conversation — we review the property and compatibility before recommending equipment.

Can you work with the equipment I already have?

Often, yes. HES starts by reviewing what you own — make, model numbers and how it's connected — before recommending anything new. Working components stay; only what holds the experience back gets replaced.
