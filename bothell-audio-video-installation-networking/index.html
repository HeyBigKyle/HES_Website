Keep the network in the AV conversation

A Bothell home audio installation may also depend on reliable connections for streaming and work. Mark the rooms where music, television and video calls compete for coverage.

Separate speed from coverage

The internet connection coming into the property and the Wi-Fi reaching each room are different parts of the system. Describe where connections drop and which fixed devices could use wired connections.

Plan routes while access is available

If a remodel or build is underway, share its scope before finishes close. HES can consider network connections alongside speaker wiring and equipment locations.

Questions

Common questions in Bothell

Do you serve Bothell?

Yes. HES is a locally owned Greater Seattle integrator serving Bothell and the surrounding area. Brian Ritchie has been designing and installing home entertainment and smart home systems here since 2003 — and in AV since 1988.

My Wi-Fi drops in some rooms — can you fix that as part of the AV project?

Usually, yes — and it’s worth fixing together. The internet coming in and the Wi-Fi reaching each room are different parts of the system. Tell us where connections drop and we’ll review coverage, wired options and network gear alongside the audio plan.

We’re mid-remodel — is it too late to add wiring?

Not if walls are still open. Share the remodel scope and drawings now and HES can consider network and speaker wiring alongside equipment locations before finishes close.

What should I send Brian before a visit?

A useful brief includes: Rooms with weak wireless coverage, Router and modem model numbers, Plans for any open-wall work. The more you include up front, the more useful the first conversation — we review the property and compatibility before recommending equipment.

Can you work with the equipment I already have?

Often, yes. HES starts by reviewing what you own — make, model numbers and how it's connected — before recommending anything new. Working components stay; only what holds the experience back gets replaced.
