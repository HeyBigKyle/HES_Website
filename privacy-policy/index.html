Privacy policy.

How we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website or get in touch about a project.

Introduction

This Privacy Policy demonstrates the commitment of Home Entertainment Solutions to protecting visitor privacy, and sets forth our information collection and management practices.

This site is for informational purposes only and does not provide legal advice. Materials on this website are published to provide visitors with free information. While we make every effort to provide accurate website information, laws can change and inaccuracies happen despite our best efforts. If you have an individual legal or medical problem, you should seek legal or medical advice from an attorney or licensed physician in your own state.

By using this website, you consent to and acknowledge all practices described below.

Information we collect

You may browse the Home Entertainment Solutions website without disclosing any personally identifiable information. We collect personally identifiable information only when you expressly consent — for example, by emailing us or submitting an information request form.

Information you provide is used to:

  • Provide our services, respond to inquiries, and handle other customer service matters.
  • Tailor the content and information we send or display, including personalized help and instructions.
  • For marketing and promotional purposes — for example, sending news, special offers, or promotions you may be interested in. We may also use information to help advertise our services on third-party websites or to administer sweepstakes, contests, and other promotions.
  • Fulfill orders — information provided when placing an order is used only to service that order, and is not shared with outside parties except as necessary to provide the service.
  • Better understand how visitors access and use the website, to improve the site and services and for other research and analytical purposes.

We also receive non-personally identifiable information through log files and cookies when you visit the site — such as IP address, page views, operating system, browser software, and referring and destination websites. If you disable or reject cookies, some areas of the website may not display properly. We do not link information gathered from cookies or log files to personally identifiable information.

Our systems do not currently recognize browser "do-not-track" requests; you may disable certain tracking as described above (for example, by disabling cookies). We are not aware of third parties that collect personally identifiable information about your online activities over time and across different websites when you visit ours.

How we share information

Home Entertainment Solutions does not share your information with third parties without your express written consent, except where disclosure is necessary to comply with the law or to protect the safety of our users or others, or with service providers performing services on our behalf — such as communication, database, event management, hosting, mailing, and marketing services. Any such service providers access your information only as necessary to perform the service on our behalf.

We have no obligation to monitor the website or retain the content of any user session, but we reserve the right at all times to monitor, review, and retain any information in order to comply with the law or protect the safety of our users or others.

If Home Entertainment Solutions is acquired by or merged with another company, if substantially all of our assets are transferred to another company, or to a third party as part of a bankruptcy proceeding, we may transfer the information collected from users to the other company or third party.

Linked sites

We may occasionally include links to external sites on our website. Inclusion of a link does not imply our endorsement of that external website or the products and services offered there. If you follow a link to a third-party site, any information you reveal on that site is not subject to this Privacy Policy.

Security

This website employs industry-standard security measures designed to protect against loss, misuse, or alteration of information you provide. We cannot fully eliminate all risks, and we are not responsible for unauthorized access by internet users with malicious intent or those who have obtained access through illegal means.

Children under 13

This website is not designed for children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information (except for information provided by a child's parent in connection with our services), we will delete it from our systems.

Changes to this policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy at any time by posting an updated policy at this location. Your continued use of the website constitutes acceptance of the policy as amended. You are responsible for reviewing this policy regularly.
