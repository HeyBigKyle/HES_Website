Questions

Common questions in Redmond

Do you serve Redmond?

Yes. HES is a locally owned Greater Seattle integrator serving Redmond and the surrounding area. Brian Ritchie has been designing and installing home entertainment and smart home systems here since 2003 — and in AV since 1988.

Do you have experience with architectural speakers in timber ceilings?

Yes — the Standley / Redmond project used architectural speakers in a timber ceiling. Send ceiling and wall photos so we can review structure, depth and cable access before recommending in-ceiling or in-wall speakers for your home.

Can different rooms play different music?

Yes. Zones follow your household’s habits: rooms used together can share music while bedrooms or offices get independent volume. List the places you listen and we’ll design zones around them.

What should I send Brian before a visit?

A useful brief includes: Rooms that should play together, Ceiling and wall photographs, Existing speaker wiring or equipment. The more you include up front, the more useful the first conversation — we review the property and compatibility before recommending equipment.

Can you work with the equipment I already have?

Often, yes. HES starts by reviewing what you own — make, model numbers and how it's connected — before recommending anything new. Working components stay; only what holds the experience back gets replaced.
